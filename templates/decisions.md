Product decisions with their reasons. Rules:

- An entry is never edited after it is accepted. A later entry either adds to it, leaving it in force, or supersedes it whole and names it on a `- Supersedes: D-nn` line.
- One entry answers one question; the parts of one answer stay together.
- "Tentative" is a chosen course that may change without a new entry. A choice the owner has not explicitly approved is tentative; accepting it changes its status and adds a line to History.
- History lists only what the entries do not show: the log created, a tentative entry accepted or withdrawn.
- An entry names where it was decided on a `- Source:` line: a call with the time of the passage, a message with its date, the owner's answer. When sources disagree, the owner's latest word wins.
- A question with a recommendation is a tentative entry, the other options rejected; open questions keep only questions without one.
- What is put off gets an entry with a `- Return when:` line that says what must happen first.
- Concepts are named by ID; the check verifies them.
