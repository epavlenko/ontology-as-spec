# Renewing a loan

<!-- ont: action.renew@3a0bfa, rule.renewal@ee5883, field.loan.renewals@d73680 -->
## How renewal works

If you need an item longer, renew it. Each renewal adds 14 days to the current due date, and the app tells you the new due date and how many of your two renewals you have used.

<!-- ont: inv.no-renew-when-held@58bed4 -->
## When renewal is refused

You cannot renew an item while another member is waiting for the same title. The app refuses and tells you that someone is waiting, so you know why. Bring the item back by its due date.
