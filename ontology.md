# Ontology as Specification — ontology of the approach

Created: 2026-10-22
Status: maintained
Source of truth for the approach's concepts. Decisions and their reasons live in [decisions.md](./decisions.md); the checks in [checks.md](./checks.md).

The approach described with itself: the concepts its artifacts are made of, the rules they follow, and how each is realised. The tables follow the same rules as any product ontology made with the approach, and `tools/ont-check/ont-check.mjs --config ont.config.json` checks them. [docs/reference.md](docs/reference.md) is generated from this file and the checks; the pages in `docs/` explain it for people.

## How to read this

The ontology is these tables and the check catalogue in [checks.md](./checks.md): the tables say what each thing is, the catalogue what must happen. One section per entity of the approach, one row per concept, rows grouped by kind in the configured order. Fields and relations name their entity. Types use the neutral vocabulary; `contracts/approach.schema.json` is generated from them.

"Implemented" has a particular meaning here. A concept is implemented when it is realised: by the tool, for what the tool enforces, or by the page of `docs/` that states it, for rules people follow and procedures they carry out. Which rules the tool cannot enforce is visible in the check catalogue: their checks are of review, which a reviewer gives a verdict reading the change, or of run, which an agent carries out on the working product.

- A verdict record is not the evidence of the check: it is a few lines that `--review` and the reviewer write, so that the check can see which checks got a verdict (D-157). `rule.manual-evidence-kept` still keeps the evidence outside the repository.
- A failed `derived.current-run` is listed as information whatever `verdicts.require` says: the key asks that the checks get a verdict, not that they pass.
- The IDs keep the word manual of their first meaning, since what they name, a record of a verdict, did not change (`inv.ids-permanent`); the section's key stays `manual-run`, so that `check.manual-run.*` stays in its section (D-157).
- The stamp of a check writes the proof `review` as the earlier `manual`, so that the rename alone makes no verdict stale; a check of run has its own stamp, and a change between review and run changes it. The stamp of a check of the approach, in a project, is its case and the IDs of its concepts, and for a check of run also its proof and the hash of its guide's section (D-157).
- `By:` names who gave the verdict and `Performer:` who carried the check out; until a later step of the approach, the only way of a verdict into a record is the reviewer's table, `--record`.
- The configuration's key was `manualRuns`; it is read as `verdicts`, and a configuration with both is an error (`rule.tool-before-files`).

- A change of the rules on GitHub by an agent with the rights of an administrator is refused by no gate; it is a limitation the owner accepted, and a later step compares the settings with the rule file.
